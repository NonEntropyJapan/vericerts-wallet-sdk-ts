# Code of Conduct

The VeriCerts Wallet SDK project adopts [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). We welcome people of all backgrounds and identities and are committed to a community where everyone can participate free from harassment.

## Community standards

- Treat others with respect and be open to different experiences and viewpoints.
- Give and receive feedback constructively. Take responsibility when your actions cause harm.
- Consider the needs of the whole community when making decisions.

Harassment, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone's private information without their consent violate this code. It applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere, including on social media and at events.

## Reporting

Use the [VeriCerts contact form](https://vericerts.co.jp/#contact) to report a concern. Start your message with `VeriCerts Wallet SDK Code of Conduct` so it can be routed to the maintainers. Include a brief description and request a private follow-up for sensitive details. Keep reports out of public issues and pull requests.

Maintainers review reports fairly, protect the reporter's privacy, and share information only as needed to respond.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content and take action proportionate to the conduct and its impact. Responses can include a private correction, a formal warning, a temporary suspension for serious or sustained violations, or a permanent ban for repeated harmful conduct.
